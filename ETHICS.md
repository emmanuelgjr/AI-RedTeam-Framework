# Ethics

This framework is a **defensive resource** for **authorized testing**.

## Hard rules

1. **Authorized use only.** Engagements require written authorization. The Rules of Engagement document (generated by the Engagement Planner) is the operational artifact for that authorization.
2. **No weaponized payloads.** The framework references public knowledge. It does not host novel exploit code, zero-days, or attack tools that primarily enable harm.
3. **Patterns are defensive in framing.** Each attack pattern is presented as "how to test defenses against," not as "how to attack." Contributions that fail this framing are rejected.
4. **No specific-target framing.** Patterns describe categories of technique; they do not target named third-party systems or named individuals.
5. **Responsible disclosure.** Novel issues identified in tools or systems referenced here follow responsible disclosure to the affected vendor or maintainer.

## Contributor sign-off

By submitting content, contributors affirm:

- The content is derived from public sources or is otherwise authorized for sharing.
- The content's primary purpose is to enable defenders, auditors, and researchers — not attackers.
- The contributor has not signed any obligation that prevents the contribution.

## Maintainer right to reject

Maintainers reserve the right to reject contributions that, in their judgment, primarily enable harm — even if technically compliant with the above.

## When in doubt

If a contribution is borderline, open an issue first to discuss before investing time in a PR.
