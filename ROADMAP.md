# Roadmap

## v0.2 (current)

Shipped in v0.1:

- 15 playbook chapters
- Engagement Planner (12-step wizard, Word ROE generation)
- 25 attack patterns
- 15 tool reviews
- Compliance crosswalk

Added in v0.2:

- 10 downloadable templates with full content — charter, ROE master, RACI
  matrix (Excel), engagement brief, daily standup (Markdown), exit
  briefing, executive summary report, audit-ready findings report,
  technical findings report, quarterly program report. Generated by
  `scripts/generate-templates.ts`.
- Metrics page with sample dashboards (inline SVG, "Sample data" badges)
- Cross-links from attack detail pages to the live AI-Controls-Catalog
- Pattern submission builder at `/attacks/submit/` with schema-mirroring
  validation, plus a PR template carrying the quality-bar checklist

## v0.3

- Per-vertical packs (banking-Canada, banking-US, healthcare, public-sector).
- Maturity self-assessment quiz.
- Walkthrough video tour.

## v1.0

- All 25 attack patterns plus 10 community contributions.
- All 15 tools annually re-reviewed.
- Compliance crosswalk: OSFI E-21, NYDFS 500, ECB SREP AI, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001 — all complete.
- Engagement Planner adoption. Note the original "≥ 100 unique ROE
  downloads tracked" target needs rethinking: the site has no telemetry by
  design, so downloads cannot be counted without breaking that commitment.
